# ReGenesis: LLMs can Grow into Reasoning Generalists via Self-Improvement

ReGenesis: 通过自我改进使大型语言模型成为推理通才

## 研究背景与动机

ReGenesis 是一种新型框架，旨在让大型语言模型(LLMs)通过自我合成推理路径来提升其通用推理能力。现有研究表明，使用显式推理轨迹进行后训练可以增强 LLMs 的推理能力，但获取高质量的推理轨迹通常需要人类或更强大模型的监督，这既昂贵又受限于许可约束。[1]

现有的自我合成方法如 STaR 和 LMSI 虽然可以改进模型的领域内推理能力，但在领域外(OOD)任务上表现不佳，实验显示这些方法在 OOD 任务上平均性能下降约 4.6%。研究者认为这是因为它们合成的推理路径过于任务特定，缺乏通用的任务无关推理指导。[1][3]

## ReGenesis 框架核心方法

ReGenesis 通过从抽象到具体的方式自我合成推理路径，具体步骤如下：

1. **指导适应**：首先创建一组通用推理指导原则，然后使用语言模型将其调整为更加任务特定的推理指导原则。[2][6]

2. **推理结构生成**：使用任务特定的推理指导原则生成详细的推理结构，这些结构提供了解决问题的框架但不直接解决问题。[2][6]

3. **推理路径生成**：利用推理结构生成候选推理路径和预测答案。[2][7]

4. **过滤推理路径**：使用正确答案或多数投票来过滤不正确的推理路径。对于缺乏正确推理路径的指令，将正确答案作为提示重新生成推理路径。[2][8]

5. **模型微调**：使用过滤后的正确推理路径、指令和答案来微调模型，增强其推理能力。[2][3]

与现有方法不同，ReGenesis 不依赖人类设计的任务特定示例，而是通过从通用到特定的推理过程生成多样化的推理路径。[3]

### Abstract

Post-training Large Language Models (LLMs) with explicit reasoning trajectoriescan enhance their reasoning abilities. However, acquiring such high-quality tra-jectory data typically demands meticulous supervision from humans or superiormodels, which can be either expensive or license-constrained. In this paper, weexplore how far an LLM can improve its reasoning by self-synthesizing reasoningpaths as training data without any additional supervision. Existing self-synthesizingmethods, such as STaR, suffer from poor generalization to out-of-domain (OOD)reasoning tasks. We hypothesize it is due to that their self-synthesized reasoningpaths are too task-specific, lacking general task-agnostic reasoning guidance. ### 2 Related Work

- **Learning from reasoning paths with supervision.** Recent research shows that employing explicitreasoning paths with extensive supervision from either humans or superior models, can lead to betterreasoning capabilities after post-training a LLM (Lightman et al., 2023; Ye et al., 2023; Yuan et al.,2024; Mukherjee et al., 2023; Achiam et al., 2023; Gou et al., 2023; Mitra et al., 2023; Qu et al., 2024;Sun et al., 2024). For example, Mukherjee et al. (2023) and Mitra et al. (2023) illustrate how smallermodels can benefit from detailed explanations and step-by-step reasoning processes provided byGPT-4 (Achiam et al., 2023). TRIPOST (Yu et al., 2023) further illustrates this by effectively trainingsmaller models to learn from mistakes and enhance their performance on mathematical and reasoningtasks with guidance from a more powerful language model like Codex (Chen et al., 2021). Yuan et al.(2024) also demonstrates that incorporating high-quality multi-turn reasoning generated by a superiormodel during training can improve the overall capabilities of LLMs. Moreover, process supervisionmethods (Lightman et al., 2023; Luo et al., 2023; Wang et al., 2024) uses extensive finer-grainedhuman annotations on step-by-step reasoning paths as post-training data. These approaches all relyon more advanced teacher models or human experts for extensive supervision to obtain high-quality,explicit intermediate reasoning paths. When more advanced models or human supervision are notavailable due to license and cost issues, we are curious to see how well LLMs can leveraging itself ongenerating reasoning paths to enhance its own reasoning capabilities.

- **基于监督的推理路径学习。**近期研究表明，使用来自人类或更高级模型的广泛监督下的显式推理路径，可以在对 LLM 进行后训练后获得更好的推理能力。例如，Mukherjee 等(2023)和 Mitra 等(2023)展示了较小的模型如何从 GPT-4 提供的详细解释和逐步推理过程中受益。TRIPOST(Yu 等，2023)通过训练较小的模型从错误中学习，并在 Codex 等更强大语言模型的指导下提高其在数学和推理任务上的表现，进一步说明了这一点。Yuan 等(2024)也证明，在训练过程中纳入由更高级模型生成的高质量多轮推理可以提升 LLMs 的整体能力。此外，过程监督方法使用人类对逐步推理路径的广泛细粒度标注作为后训练数据。这些方法都依赖于更高级的教师模型或人类专家的广泛监督来获取高质量的显式中间推理路径。当由于许可和成本问题无法获得更高级的模型或人类监督时，我们好奇 LLMs 在多大程度上能够利用自身生成推理路径来增强其推理能力。

- **Learning from self-synthesized reasoning paths.** Contrary to the large number of methods abovewhich rely on external supervision to create reasoning paths for post-training, there are limited existingwork that employs self-synthesized reasoning paths from a LLM to improve its own reasoning capacity.Self-Taught Reasoner (STaR) (Zelikman et al., 2022) improves the reasoning capabilities of languagemodels using self-generated, ground-truth-filtered reasoning paths which are prompted with a fewhuman-written CoT examples. Huang et al. (2023) trains language models with self-generatedreasoning paths filtered by self-consistent answers, also aided by human-written examples. However,we observed that they fail to improve the given LLMs on OOD tasks through our experiments whileour framework improves model performance across both in domain and OOD reasoning tasks.

- **基于自我合成推理路径的学习。**与上述大量依赖外部监督来创建后训练推理路径的方法相比，很少有现有工作使用 LLM 自我合成的推理路径来提升其推理能力。自我教学推理器(STaR)(Zelikman 等，2022)使用经过真实标签过滤的自生成推理路径来提升语言模型的推理能力，这些路径是通过少量人工编写的 CoT 示例提示生成的。Huang 等(2023)使用经过自一致性答案过滤的自生成推理路径训练语言模型，同样借助人工编写的示例。然而，通过我们的实验，我们观察到它们未能在 OOD 任务上改进给定的 LLMs，而我们的框架则在领域内和 OOD 推理任务上都提高了模型性能。

- **Diverse prompting methods.** Recent research has greatly improved the reasoning capability ofLLMs through more effective prompting techniques (Zhou et al., 2022; Kojima et al., 2022; Zhenget al., 2023). For example, Wei et al. (2022) introduced CoT prompting, where the model generates aseries of intermediate reasoning steps, making it easier to solve complex, multi-step reasoning tasks.Wang et al. (2022) enhanced this approach by sampling multiple different reasoning paths and thenusing majority voting to identify the most consistent answers, referred as Self-Consistency. Zhou et al.(2024) further developed these techniques by enabling LLMs to select and combine simple reasoningmodules—like critical thinking and step-by-step analysis—into a clear reasoning structure during decoding. These prompting methods, particularly those described by Zhou et al. ### 3 ReGenesis: Reasoning Generalist via Self-Improvement

Existing methods have demonstrated that we can self-improve LLM’s reasoning performance inin-domain settings by self-synthesizing reasoning paths, as also verified by our experiments in Section4.2. However, when applying existing methods in out-of-domain (OOD) settings, we have found thatthey lead to non-neglectable performance drop, shown in Section 4.3. We hypothesize that the mainreason for previous methods being unable to generalize to OOD tasks is that their self-synthesizedreasoning paths are too task-specific, lacking general task-agnostic reasoning guidance and diversity.Therefore, to verify this hypothesis and to provide a self-improving method that can help LLMs togeneralize well on diverse reasoning tasks after training LLMs on limited reasoning tasks, we proposeReGenesis. ReGenesis adopts a strategy to self-synthesize reasoning paths by progressing fromabstract to concrete, from general reasoning structures to task-specific reasoning paths. Building onthis idea, ReGenesis can automatically generate and integrate reasoning paths into post-trainingdatasets that originally contain only instructions and ground-truth answers. Figure 1 shows how wecreate self-synthesized reasoning paths for a task. We start with a trained LLM M and a trainingdataset Dtrain = {qi, ai}|D|i=1, where qi is a text instruction and ai is the corresponding ground-truthsolution. All system prompts we used during this process are shown in Appendix A.3. The goal ofReGenesis is to use M to generate reasoning paths as post-training data to fine-tune M, to furtherimprove M’s performance on both in-domain and OOD reasoning tasks.

现有方法已经证明，我们可以通过自我合成推理路径来提升 LLM 在领域内环境下的推理性能，这一点也在我们第 4.2 节的实验中得到验证。然而，当在领域外（OOD）环境中应用现有方法时，我们发现它们会导致不可忽视的性能下降，这在第 4.3 节中有所展示。我们假设先前方法无法泛化到 OOD 任务的主要原因是，它们自我合成的推理路径过于任务特定，缺乏通用的任务无关推理指导和多样性。

因此，为了验证这一假设，并提供一种自我改进方法，帮助 LLMs 在有限推理任务训练后能够在 diverse 推理任务上良好泛化，我们提出了 ReGenesis。ReGenesis 采用从抽象到具体、从通用推理结构到任务特定推理路径的策略来自我合成推理路径。基于这一理念，ReGenesis 可以自动生成推理路径并将其整合到原本只包含指令和真实答案的后训练数据集中。图 1 展示了我们如何为一个任务创建自我合成的推理路径。我们从一个训练好的 LLM M 和训练数据集 $D^{train}={q_i,q_i}^{abs(D)}_{i=1}$ 开始，其中$q_i$是文本指令，$a_i$是相应的真实解决方案。我们在这个过程中使用的所有系统提示都在附录 A.3 中展示。ReGenesis 的目标是使用 M 生成推理路径作为后训练数据来微调 M，以进一步提高 M 在领域内和 OOD 推理任务上的性能。

#### 3.1 Self-Synthesized Reasoning Paths

Guidance Adaption. We first create a set of the seed general reasoning guidelines Praw = {prawj }|P|j=1, which provide general descriptions on problem-solving strategies which aim at activating thelanguage model’s reasoning capabilities. These prompts are designed to be broad and applicableto a wide range of different tasks. For example, “How can I break down this problem into smaller,more manageable parts?”. The full list of seed guidelines can be found in Appendix A.3.1. For eachinstruction qi, we use M to edit and expand each seed guideline, tailoring it more specifically to thetask, thereby generating an adapted task-specific reasoning guidelines Padpi,j = {padpi,j }|P|j=1.

As demonstrated in Figure 1, “Make a list of ideas for solving this problem” in one of the seedguidelines can be tailored for a salary calculation task to: “Make a list of ideas for understandinghourly wage and overtime rules, and systematically apply each idea to accurately calculate wages.”In this step, for each instruction qi, we generate an adapted guidance for every corresponding seedguidance, resulting in a total of |P| adapted prompts per instruction.

**指导适配。** 我们首先创建一组种子通用推理指导原则 Praw = {prawj }|P|j=1，这些原则提供了解决问题策略的通用描述，目的是激活语言模型的推理能力。这些提示被设计为广泛且适用于各种不同任务。

例如：

> "如何将这个问题分解成更小、更易管理的部分？"

完整的种子指导原则列表可以在附录 A.3.1 中找到。

对于每个指令 qi，我们使用模型 M 来编辑和扩展每个种子指导原则，使其更具体地针对任务，从而生成适配后的特定任务推理指导原则 Padpi,j = {padpi,j }|P|j=1。

如图 1 所示，种子指导原则中的"列出解决这个问题的想法清单"可以针对工资计算任务进行调整，变为："列出理解时薪和加班规则的想法清单，并系统地应用每个想法来准确计算工资。"

在这一步中，对于每个指令 qi，我们为每个对应的种子指导生成一个适配后的指导，从而为每条指令生成总共|P|个适配后的提示。

Reasoning Structure Generation. We prompt the same language model M to develop a setof detailed reasoning structures Rsi = {rsi,j}|P|j=1 for each instruction, qi, utilizing the adaptedaccordingly. Specifically, for each adapted prompt padpi,j , the model M generates onecorresponding reasoning structure rsi,j. Instead of developing a detailed reasoning path directly, thereasoning structures convert the adapted prompts into a more detailed framework without solvingthe problem. Figure 1 illustrates how the adapted prompt for the salary calculation task can betransformed into a practical reasoning structure, without directly solving the entire task. For example,“1) Understand the hourly wage and overtime rules, 2) Determine the number of shifts, ...” Thisreasoning structure serves as a thinking principle, bridging the gap between the general seed promptsand the detailed reasoning paths necessary to complete the task.

我们提示相同的语言模型 M，利用适配后的指导为每个指令 qi 开发一组详细的推理结构 Rsi = {rsi,j}|P|j=1。

具体来说，对于每个适配后的提示 padpi,j，模型 M 生成一个对应的推理结构 rsi,j。推理结构不直接开发详细的推理路径，而是将适配后的提示转换为更详细的框架，但不直接解决问题。

图 1 展示了如何将工资计算任务的适配后提示转换为实用的推理结构，而不直接解决整个任务。例如：

> "1) 理解时薪和加班规则 2) 确定班次数量..."

这个推理结构作为思维原则，架起了通用种子提示和完成任务所需的详细推理路径之间的桥梁。

Reasoning Paths Generation. Each reasoning structure rsi,j is used to generate a potential reasoningpath and predicted answer (rpathi,j , ˆai,j) for a given instruction qi. The reasoning path addressesinstruction qi step by step with the reasoning structure, thereby providing robust data for subsequentfine-tuning. For example, as shown in Figure 1, the reasoning structure for calculating salary isdeveloped into a detailed reasoning path, “1) Tina’s regular hourly wage is $18.00 per hour, 2) Tina works 10 hours every day, ...”, with the predicted answer $990.00. After this process, we obtain atotal of |P| reasoning solutions per instruction. The ablation study is presented in Section 5.1.

每个推理结构 rsi,j 用于为给定指令 qi 生成潜在的推理路径和预测答案(rpathi,j, ˆai,j)。推理路径按照推理结构逐步解决指令 qi，从而为后续微调提供可靠数据。

例如，如图 1 所示，计算工资的推理结构被发展成详细的推理路径：

> "1) Tina 的正常时薪是每小时 18.00 美元 2) Tina....每天工作 10 小时..."，预测答案为 990.00 美元。

经过这个过程，我们为每条指令获得了总共|P|个推理解决方案。消融研究的结果在第 5.1 节中呈现。

#### 3.2 Filtering Reasoning Paths

**Filtering with the Ground-truth.** Same as previous methods such as Zelikman et al. (2022) thatgenerate reasoning paths given prompts and ground-truth answers of existing data sets, we use thegiven ground-truth answers to filter our self-synthesized reasoning paths. Due to the limitationsin the reasoning capabilities of language models, there is no guarantee that the predicted solutionˆai,j using the reasoning path is the same as the given answer ai. Therefore, we use ai to filter outthose reasoning solutions that are incorrect, assuming that only reasoning paths leading to the correctanswer are valid. Additionally, we explore self-consistency as an alternative method for selectingvalid reasoning paths in Section 5.2.

**使用真实答案进行筛选。**与 Zelikman 等人(2022)等之前的方法类似，在给定提示和现有数据集的真实答案的情况下生成推理路径时，我们使用给定的真实答案来筛选我们的自我合成推理路径。由于语言模型在推理能力上的限制，无法保证使用推理路径得到的预测解答 ˆai,j 与给定答案 ai 相同。因此，我们使用 ai 来筛选出那些不正确的推理解决方案，假设只有导向正确答案的推理路径才是有效的。此外，我们在第 5.2 节中探索了使用自我一致性作为选择有效推理路径的替代方法。

**Adding Ground-truth as a Hint.** For instruction qi that doesnot have any correct reasoning solutions after filtering, we in-clude the ground-truth ai as a hint to generate adapted guidanceand reasoning structures (system prompts can be found in Ap-pendix A.3.3). Unlike Zelikman et al. (2022) which provideshints directly in the final reasoning path generation process, weonly add hints during “Guidance Adaption” step and “ReasoningStructure Generation” step. By not having ground-truth answersin the final reasoning generation stage, we reduce the risk of thefinal reasoning solutions producing a correct answer but havingan incorrect reasoning path. We show the effectiveness of thisdesign choice in Section 5.3. Additionally, incorporating groundtruth as a hint significantly improved the utilization of the trainingset, increasing the average usage from 96.2% to 99.4% across 5datasets, as shown in Table 1.Finally, for each dataset Dtrain, we create a new dataset incorporating reasoning paths, denoted asDtrain′ =nqi, rpath`i,j , aio|D|,|<P|i=1,j=1`, where rpathi,j represents the correct reasoning path after filtering.We randomly select up to p correct reasoning paths for each instruction qi. The language model M isthen fine-tuned using the input qi and the target outputs {rpathi,j , ai}.

**添加真实答案作为提示。**对于在筛选后没有任何正确推理解决方案的指令 qi，我们将真实答案 ai 作为提示来生成适配指导和推理结构（系统提示可在附录 A.3.3 中找到）。与 Zelikman 等人(2022)的方法不同，他们在最终推理路径生成过程中直接提供提示，而我们只在"指导适配"步骤和"推理结构生成"步骤中添加提示。通过在最终推理生成阶段不使用真实答案，我们降低了最终推理解决方案产生正确答案但推理路径不正确的风险。我们在第 5.3 节中展示了这种设计选择的有效性。此外，将真实答案作为提示显著提高了训练集的利用率，如表 1 所示，在 5 个数据集中的平均使用率从 96.2%提升到 99.4%。

最后，对于每个数据集 Dtrain，我们创建一个包含推理路径的新数据集，表示为：
`Dtrain′ = {qi, rpathi,j, ai}|D|,|<P|i=1,j=1` 其中 rpathi,j 代表筛选后的正确推理路径。我们为每个指令 qi 随机选择最多 p 个正确的推理路径。然后使用输入 qi 和目标输出{rpathi,j, ai}对语言模型 M 进行微调。

## Sidenote

STaR (Self-Taught Reasoner) 是一种自我教学推理器方法，由 Zelikman 等人在 2022 年提出。这是一个用于改进语言模型推理能力的方法，其主要特点如下：

1. **工作原理**：

   - 让语言模型自己生成推理步骤（思维链）
   - 使用最终答案的正确性来筛选这些推理步骤
   - 用筛选后的高质量推理样本来微调模型

2. **核心思想**：

   - 不依赖外部监督（如人工标注或更强大的模型）
   - 模型通过"自我教学"来提升推理能力
   - 利用思维链（Chain-of-Thought）方法来展示推理过程

3. **局限性**（根据原文描述）：
   - 在领域外(OOD)任务上泛化能力较差
   - 生成的推理路径过于特定于训练任务
   - 缺乏多样性的推理模式

STaR 代表了一种重要的自我改进范式，但正如文章所指出的，它在处理领域外任务时存在明显的局限性，这也是后来 ReGenesis 方法试图解决的问题。

---

思维链（Chain-of-Thought，简称 CoT）是一种提示语言模型进行推理的方法，由 Wei 等人在 2022 年提出。这是一种让语言模型像人类一样"一步步思考"的技术。

# 主要特点

1. **基本概念**

   - 不是直接给出答案，而是展示完整的推理过程
   - 模仿人类解决问题时的思维步骤
   - 每个步骤都清晰可见，逻辑连贯

2. **示例说明**

```
问题：小明有5个苹果，给了小红3个，又从商店买了2个，现在小明有几个苹果？

传统回答：
4个苹果

思维链回答：
1. 首先，小明有5个苹果
2. 给了小红3个，所以剩下 5-3=2 个苹果
3. 从商店买了2个，所以现在有 2+2=4 个苹果
4. 因此，小明现在有4个苹果
```

3. **优势**

   - 提高模型推理的准确性
   - 使推理过程可解释
   - 有助于发现推理中的错误
   - 更接近人类的思维方式

4. **应用场景**
   - 数学问题解决
   - 逻辑推理
   - 常识推理
   - 多步骤决策问题

思维链方法的提出是语言模型发展的一个重要里程碑，它使模型能够展示出更接近人类的推理能力，而不是简单的"问答"模式。
