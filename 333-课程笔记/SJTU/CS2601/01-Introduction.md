## Mathematical Optimization

### What is Optimization

优化就是在多种选择中挑选最好的一种。任何存在决策的问题都是优化问题。

- the selection of a **best** element
- with regard to some **criteria**
- from some set of available alternatives

### Common Applications

- Minimal Cost
- Maximal Profit
- Minimal Error
- Optimal Design
- Optimal Management
- Variational Principles
