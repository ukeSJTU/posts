Exercise 9 — Tasks

1.  List all movies and their combined sales in **millions** of dollars
2.  List all movies and their ratings **in percent**
3.  List all movies that were released on even number years

---

# Answer

```sql
SELECT title, (domestic_sales + international_sales) / 1000000 AS sales FROM movies INNER JOIN boxoffice ON id=movie_id;
SELECT title, rating * 10 AS rating_percent FROM movies INNER JOIN boxoffice ON id=movie_id;
SELECT title FROM movies WHERE year%2=0;
```
