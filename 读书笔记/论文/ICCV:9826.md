## Abstract

尽管视频语言模型蓬勃发展，但当前对全面视频推理的追求受到了个体视频固有的时空不完整性的阻碍，导致幻觉和不准确性。[1] 一个有前景的解决方案是通过多个相关视频来增强推理性能。[1] 然而，视频令牌数量庞大且包含冗余信息，因此直接将相关视频数据输入大型语言模型以增强响应可能会适得其反。[1][2] 为了应对这一挑战，我们提出了一个视频语言模型的多视频协作框架。[2] 为了实现高效灵活的视频表示，我们建立了一个视频结构化模块，将视频的知识表示为时空图。[2] 基于结构化的视频表示，我们设计了图融合模块，将相关视频的结构化知识和有价值信息融合到增强的图节点令牌中。[2] 最后，我们构建了一个精心设计的多视频结构化提示，将图形、视觉和文本令牌整合为大型语言模型的输入。[2][3] 大量实验证实了我们框架的有效性，展示了其作为推进视频语言模型的一个有前景途径的潜力。[3]

## 1. Introduction

The significant advancement in Large Language Models(LLMs) gives rise to the prosperity of video language models. Equipped with LLMs' strong general knowledge, video language models showcase an impressive potential to comprehend complex video contents [24, 27, 28, 34, 36, 57, 60]. These models effectively bridge the semantic chasm between video and language, facilitating a nuanced integration of visual perception with language processing.

大型语言模型(LLMs)的显著进步促成了视频语言模型的繁荣发展。借助 LLMs 强大的通用知识，视频语言模型展示出理解复杂视频内容的令人印象深刻的潜力[24, 27, 28, 34, 36, 57, 60]。这些模型有效地弥合了视频和语言之间的语义鸿沟，促进了视觉感知与语言处理的细微整合。

However, it remains an elusive goal to achieve reliable and comprehensive video reasoning. Specifically, the spatio-temporal knowledge captured from an individual video is often incomplete due to the limited visual perception and comprehending ability. Factors hindering visual perception include sparse sampling [30, 38, 56, 58], spatial occlusion [9, 20], and perspective switching [12, 42, 48, 62]). The comprehending ability is mainly constrained by the complexity of video content [18, 53] and limited effective context length [17, 32]. Since video language models mostly originate from large language models [19, 40] with the help of visual instruction tuning [31], video language models can only resort to the language prior [13, 29] when faced with knowledge incompleteness. This tendency brings spurious hallucination and misalignment with the visual facts [14, 26] to the answers.

然而，实现可靠和全面的视频推理仍然是一个难以捉摸的目标。具体来说，从单个视频中捕获的时空知识由于有限的视觉感知和理解能力而常常不完整。阻碍视觉感知的因素包括稀疏采样[30, 38, 56, 58]、空间遮挡[9, 20]和视角切换[12, 42, 48, 62]。理解能力主要受到视频内容复杂性[18, 53]和有效上下文长度限制[17, 32]的约束。由于视频语言模型大多源自大型语言模型[19, 40]，并借助视觉指令调优[31]，当面临知识不完整时，视频语言模型只能求助于语言先验[13, 29]。这种倾向使答案产生虚假幻觉和与视觉事实[14, 26]的不一致。

The mainstream of video language models follows a single-video reasoning pipeline (shown in Figure 1(a)). Motivated by the research in multi-data collaboration [46, 51, 52, 63] and retrieval-augmented generation [11, 15, 22], a promising idea to address this limitation is integrating knowledge from multiple related videos. We illustrate some video question-answering examples in Figure 2. As the examples demonstrate, single-video reasoning suffers from incomplete and redundant information from an individual video. These challenges prevent video language models from grasping valuable knowledge and lead to hallucinations and failures in answering the given questions. Introducing multiple highly related videos within the same domain can compensate for the missing information and enable the video language models to conclude and provide reliable answers.

主流视频语言模型遵循单视频推理流程(如图 1(a)所示)。受多数据协作[46, 51, 52, 63]和检索增强生成[11, 15, 22]研究的启发，解决这一限制的一个有前景的想法是整合来自多个相关视频的知识。我们在图 2 中展示了一些视频问答示例。正如这些示例所显示的，单视频推理受到来自单个视频的不完整和冗余信息的影响。这些挑战阻止视频语言模型把握有价值的知识，并导致在回答给定问题时出现幻觉和失败。引入同一领域内的多个高度相关的视频可以弥补缺失的信息，并使视频语言模型能够得出结论并提供可靠的答案。

Despite the apparent advantages, the incremental information from multiple videos does not necessarily lead to better reasoning in real cases. As illustrated in Figure 1(b), A direct multi-video joint strategy is to concatenate the visual features of multiple videos in the prompt before passing them to the video language models, which can bring an overwhelming number of tokens to video language models [24, 28, 36]. When exposed to long context, empirical evidence [17, 32] proves that LLMs are inclined to concentrate only on specific and limited segments of the input, neglecting potentially critical information. Moreover, the high dimensionality, redundancy, and obfuscation of video content contrast sharply with the structured, clear, and rule-based nature of language. This temporal visual complexity makes the synthesis and refinement of multi-video knowledge particularly challenging.

尽管有明显的优势，但来自多个视频的增量信息并不一定会在实际案例中带来更好的推理。如图 1(b)所示，一种直接的多视频联合策略是在将多个视频的视觉特征拼接到提示中，然后再将它们传递给视频语言模型，这可能会给视频语言模型带来大量 token[24, 28, 36]。当暴露于长上下文时，经验证据[17, 32]证明 LLMs 倾向于仅集中在输入的特定和有限部分，忽略潜在的关键信息。此外，视频内容的高维度、冗余性和模糊性与语言的结构化、清晰和基于规则的性质形成鲜明对比。这种时间视觉复杂性使多视频知识的综合和精炼变得特别具有挑战性。

In this paper, we present a structured multi-video collaborative reasoning framework. As illustrated in Figure 1(c), Our framework achieves the alignment and completion of multi-video information at the graph-structured representation level. Under this framework, to address the redundancy and complexity of video information, we first design the Video Structuring Module (VSM). This module leverages detection and tracking to extract key targets and their spatio-temporal relationships, thereby constructing a data-efficient structured video representation. After obtaining the structured representation, we propose the Graph Fusion Module (GFM) to yield LLM-friendly graph tokens. GFM integrates the structural information into node features through the graph attention network (GAT) and then fuses the multi-video knowledge based on cross-graph attention. Finally, we arrange the fused graph tokens, target video tokens, and text tokens with an elaborately designed prompt to augment VLMs with the multi-video structured knowledge. Overall, our contributions are three-fold:

在本文中，我们提出了一个结构化多视频协作推理框架。如图 1(c)所示，我们的框架在图结构表示层面实现了多视频信息的对齐和完成。在这个框架下，为了解决视频信息的冗余和复杂性，我们首先设计了视频结构化模块(VSM)。该模块利用检测和跟踪提取关键目标及其时空关系，从而构建数据高效的结构化视频表示。获得结构化表示后，我们提出了图融合模块(GFM)来生成对 LLM 友好的图 token。GFM 通过图注意力网络(GAT)将结构信息整合到节点特征中，然后基于跨图注意力融合多视频知识。最后，我们通过精心设计的提示，将融合的图 token、目标视频 token 和文本 token 排列组合，用多视频结构化知识增强 VLMs。总体而言，我们的贡献有三个方面：

• We explore a feasible structured reasoning framework for multi-video collaboration on video language models.
• Under this framework, we develop the Video Structuring Module to obtain the data-efficient spatio-temporal graph for video information. Then, we further design the Graph Fusion Module to fuse the spatio-temporal relations and cross-video knowledge into the graph tokens.
• Extensive experiments show that our framework can boost the reliability and accuracy through multi-video structuring and collaboration.

• 我们探索了一种可行的结构化推理框架，用于视频语言模型上的多视频协作。
• 在这个框架下，我们开发了视频结构化模块，为视频信息获取数据高效的时空图。然后，我们进一步设计了图融合模块，将时空关系和跨视频知识融合到图 token 中。
• 大量实验表明，我们的框架可以通过多视频结构化和协作提高可靠性和准确性。

To retrieve related videos, their feature vectors are constructed in advance for efficient video retrieval. Different ways of video vectorization are discussed in supplementary materials. ### 3.2. Video Structuring Module

Efficient structured video representation paves the way for the following integration of multi-video knowledge. Given the video and its paired dense caption, the process of our Video Structuring Module (VSM) is depicted on the right bottom of Figure 3, which can be specified as follows.

高效的结构化视频表示为后续多视频知识的整合铺平了道路。给定视频及其配对的密集描述，我们的视频结构化模块(VSM)的过程如图 3 右下方所示，具体如下。

Step 1: Scene detection. To avoid the video's temporal redundancy, we use a light-weighted content-based scene detector PySceneDetect [8] to detect scenes from the video. Then, the middle frame of each scene is extracted as the keyframes for the later video structuring process.

步骤 1：场景检测。为了避免视频的时间冗余，我们使用轻量级的基于内容的场景检测器 PySceneDetect [8]从视频中检测场景。然后，提取每个场景的中间帧作为后续视频结构化过程的关键帧。

Step 2: Dense video captioning. To prepare for the latter structuring pipeline, we need to extract the detailed and fine-grained textual concept. To achieve this goal, we adopt a video language model to give a detailed description of the input video through prompt design, which is detailed in our supplementary materials.

步骤 2：密集视频描述。为了准备后续的结构化流程，我们需要提取详细和细粒度的文本概念。为了实现这一目标，我们采用视频语言模型通过提示设计给输入视频提供详细描述，这在我们的补充材料中有详细说明。

Step 3: Textual scene graph parsing. We then extract the textual scene graph GText from the dense video caption with the help of the SceneGraphParser [50]. The textual scene graph is composed of several triplets τi = {si, pi, oi}, where τi represents the i-th triplets and si, pi, oi represents the subject, predicate and object of τi. Triplets are fundamental objects of interaction and event within the video, formatted in 〈subject - predicate - object〉.

步骤 3：文本场景图解析。然后，我们在 SceneGraphParser [50]的帮助下从密集视频描述中提取文本场景图 GText。文本场景图由几个三元组 τi = {si, pi, oi}组成，其中 τi 表示第 i 个三元组，si, pi, oi 分别表示 τi 的主语、谓语和宾语。三元组是视频中交互和事件的基本对象，格式为〈主语-谓语-宾语〉。

Step 4: Graph-guided grounding. Based on the textual scene graph GText, we can detect the interacted objects using the open-set detector GroundingDINO [33]. Then, we use the tracking method DEVA [5] to establish the tracklets of the former grounded objects, which temporally connect the same objects. Through detecting and tracking, we can get the: 1) grounding results B = {(bsi, boi)|1 ≤ i ≤ M}, where bsi, boi are the bounding boxes of si and oi, and 2) tracking results T = {Tidsi, Tidoi}, where Tidsi, Tidoi are the tracking ID of si and oi. Notice that triplets without grounded objects are redundant and should be filtered.

步骤 4：图引导的接地。基于文本场景图 GText，我们可以使用开放集检测器 GroundingDINO [33]检测交互对象。然后，我们使用跟踪方法 DEVA [5]建立先前接地对象的轨迹，这些轨迹在时间上连接相同的对象。通过检测和跟踪，我们可以获得：1)接地结果 B = {(bsi, boi)|1 ≤ i ≤ M}，其中 bsi, boi 是 si 和 oi 的边界框，以及 2)跟踪结果 T = {Tidsi, Tidoi}，其中 Tidsi, Tidoi 是 si 和 oi 的跟踪 ID。注意，没有接地对象的三元组是冗余的，应该被过滤掉。

Step 5: Video graph establishment. Built upon B, T and GText, we establish the graph representation for both the target video and related videos. Our graph consists of the nodes, intra-frame edges and inter-frame edges. The node represents the features of the object in the video. To get the node-level features, we use the detecting results B to crop them from the keyframes and use OpenCLIP [6] to extract their visual features. Specifically, we only utilize the [CLS] token as the output. Since the regions are pre-cropped, the [CLS] token already retains a fine-grained feature representation, eliminating the need to use all output tokens. This significantly reduces the number of tokens required to represent the visual modality. The intra-frame edge is represented by the predicates of each triplet, which indicates the spatial and interacting relationships between objects within one frame. The inter-frame edge connects objects in different frames with the same tracking ID, indicating their temporal relationships. Through the above steps, we can establish the graph-structured representation of both the target video and its related videos for further collaboration.

步骤 5：视频图建立。基于 B、T 和 GText，我们为目标视频和相关视频建立图表示。我们的图由节点、帧内边和帧间边组成。节点表示视频中对象的特征。为了获取节点级特征，我们使用检测结果 B 从关键帧中裁剪它们，并使用 OpenCLIP [6]提取它们的视觉特征。具体来说，我们只使用[CLS] token 作为输出。由于区域是预先裁剪的，[CLS] token 已经保留了细粒度的特征表示，消除了使用所有输出 token 的需要。这显著减少了表示视觉模态所需的 token 数量。帧内边由每个三元组的谓语表示，表示一帧内对象之间的空间和交互关系。帧间边连接不同帧中具有相同跟踪 ID 的对象，表示它们的时间关系。通过上述步骤，我们可以建立目标视频及其相关视频的图结构表示，以便进一步协作。

### 3.3. Graph Fusion Module

The Graph Fusion Module (GFM) is a multi-layer stacked architecture (see Figure 3), with each layer comprising two key components: the Hierarchical Frame Graph Attention Network (HF-GAT) [41] and the Cross-Graph Attention (CGA) mechanism.

图融合模块(GFM)是一个多层堆叠架构(见图 3)，每一层包含两个关键组件：层次化帧图注意力网络(HF-GAT) [41]和跨图注意力(CGA)机制。

HF-GAT is tailored to fuse graph-structured data within a single video. Traditional Graph Attention Networks (GATs) are primarily used for tasks such as node classification and relation prediction, where relationships between nodes are explicitly defined. In contrast, for both inter-frame and intra-frame contexts, these relationships are often implicit or absent. To address this challenge, as introduced in Section 3.2, we propose converting the original visual modality data into a graph-structured representation. In this graph, nodes represent features of subjects or objects, which are extracted using OpenCLIP [6]. For intra-frame edges, we utilize relationships based on triplets, where a directional link is formed from si to oi. For inter-frame edges, we leverage the results from the tracker T to link st−1i or ot−1i from the previous frame to sti or oti in the current frame. Additionally, we introduce bidirectional links for inter-frame connections, as it enhances the system's ability to comprehend video content [39].

HF-GAT 专为融合单个视频内的图结构数据而设计。传统的图注意力网络(GATs)主要用于节点分类和关系预测等任务，其中节点之间的关系是明确定义的。相比之下，对于帧间和帧内上下文，这些关系通常是隐含的或缺失的。为了解决这一挑战，如第 3.2 节所介绍的，我们提出将原始视觉模态数据转换为图结构表示。在这个图中，节点表示主语或宾语的特征，这些特征使用 OpenCLIP [6]提取。对于帧内边，我们利用基于三元组的关系，其中从 si 到 oi 形成一个定向链接。对于帧间边，我们利用跟踪器 T 的结果将前一帧的 st−1i 或 ot−1i 链接到当前帧的 sti 或 oti。此外，我们为帧间连接引入双向链接，因为它增强了系统理解视频内容的能力[39]。

Once the structured features from individual videos are extracted using HF-GAT, the next step is to select the most relevant information from related videos for the target video. To accomplish this, we introduce the cross-graph attention mechanism. Specifically, we use the target video Xtar as the query Qtar = XtarWQ, while the concatenation of the target video and related videos along the frame dimension serves as the key and value, i.e. [Vtar, Vrel] = [Xtar, Xrel]WV and [Ktar, Krel] = [Xtar, Xrel]WK, where [, ] denotes concatenation operation along the frame dimension. The concatenation order of related videos is unordered and interchangeable, so we do not explicitly introduce positional encoding, drawing inspiration from the Set Transformer [21]. The positional encoding within individual videos is implicitly provided by the structured information embedded in the data, as the input to the cross-graph attention mechanism is derived from the output of HF-GAT.

一旦使用 HF-GAT 提取了单个视频的结构化特征，下一步是为目标视频从相关视频中选择最相关的信息。为了实现这一点，我们引入了跨图注意力机制。具体来说，我们使用目标视频 Xtar 作为查询 Qtar = XtarWQ，而目标视频和相关视频沿帧维度的拼接作为键和值，即[Vtar, Vrel] = [Xtar, Xrel]WV 和[Ktar, Krel] = [Xtar, Xrel]WK，其中[, ]表示沿帧维度的拼接操作。相关视频的拼接顺序是无序且可互换的，因此我们不显式引入位置编码，这受到了 Set Transformer [21]的启发。单个视频内的位置编码由嵌入在数据中的结构化信息隐式提供，因为跨图注意力机制的输入是从 HF-GAT 的输出派生的。

Furthermore, we use class embedding (CE) to enable the cross-graph attention mechanism to distinguish between the graph of the target video and the graphs of related videos.

此外，我们使用类嵌入(CE)使跨图注意力机制能够区分目标视频的图和相关视频的图。

CEtar = σ(α), CErel = 1 − σ(α), (1)

where α represents a learnable parameter shared across frames. We then apply CE directly to the query, key, and value by adding it to each component:

其中 α 表示在帧之间共享的可学习参数。然后，我们通过将 CE 直接添加到每个组件，将其应用于查询、键和值：

ˆQtar = Qtar + CEtar, ˆKrel = Krel + CErel, ˆVrel = Vrel + CErel. (2)

ˆQtar = Qtar + CEtar, ˆKrel = Krel + CErel, ˆVrel = Vrel + CErel. (2)

By leveraging CE, the cross-graph attention mechanism implicitly learns to distinguish between the target video and the related videos. The final output of the cross-graph attention is produced by concatenating Otar, the fused representation of the target video, with Xrel, the original representation of the related videos that are initially fed into the attention mechanism.

通过利用 CE，跨图注意力机制隐式学习区分目标视频和相关视频。跨图注意力的最终输出是通过将 Otar(目标视频的融合表示)与 Xrel(最初输入到注意力机制的相关视频的原始表示)拼接而成。

We apply residual connections and pre-norm for both the HF-GAT and cross-graph attention components. Specifically, we use RMSNorm [40], which is commonly employed in large language models (LLMs), for pre-norm. Notably, we omit the Feed-Forward Network (FFN) in this layer to maintain the alignment of features without excessive shifting. The graph-structured representations of the videos are then processed through the GFM to construct graph tokens, each corresponding to the features of subjects or objects after being fused with the structured information.

我们对 HF-GAT 和跨图注意力组件都应用残差连接和预归一化。具体来说，我们使用 RMSNorm [40]进行预归一化，这在大型语言模型(LLMs)中常用。值得注意的是，我们在这一层中省略了前馈网络(FFN)，以在不过度偏移的情况下保持特征的对齐。视频的图结构表示然后通过 GFM 处理，构建图 token，每个 token 对应于与结构化信息融合后的主语或宾语的特征。

### 3.4. Structured Multi-Video Prompt

Upon obtaining the fused multi-video graph tokens, we need to integrate the graph, video, and text tokens together to create an LLM-friendly prompt. Therefore, we propose the structured multi-video prompt, as is shown in Figure 4. Our prompt originates from previous video language models' prompt design [28]. For target video, we maintain the video tokens <VIDEO TOKENS> of the target video to preserve fine-grained and detailed visual information. We also append its graph-structured data <GRAPH TOKENS> to indicate the key object and spatio-temporal relationships. For N related videos, we only maintain the concise and data-efficient graph-structured data <GRAPH TOKENS>. In this context, we further indicate the relationships between the target video and related videos and how the LLMs can utilize these related multi-video structured data. Moreover, we introduce Chain-of-Thought (CoT) [49] reasoning through the fixed prompt "Think through the process step by step". This approach helps the model to sequentially reason through structured information, improving its ability to comprehend and process the graph-based features. By structuring the prompt in this manner, we enable the video language model to leverage multi-video information in an effective way, thereby enhancing the model's ability to reason about and answer queries related to the video content.

获得融合的多视频图 token 后，我们需要将图形、视频和文本 token 整合在一起，创建一个对 LLM 友好的提示。因此，我们提出了结构化多视频提示，如图 4 所示。我们的提示源自先前视频语言模型的提示设计[28]。对于目标视频，我们保留目标视频的视频 token <VIDEO TOKENS>以保留细粒度和详细的视觉信息。我们还附加其图结构数据<GRAPH TOKENS>以指示关键对象和时空关系。对于 N 个相关视频，我们只保留简洁和数据高效的图结构数据<GRAPH TOKENS>。在这种情况下，我们进一步指出目标视频和相关视频之间的关系，以及 LLMs 如何利用这些相关的多视频结构化数据。此外，我们通过固定提示"逐步思考过程"引入思维链(CoT) [49]推理。这种方法帮助模型顺序地通过结构化信息进行推理，提高其理解和处理基于图的特征的能力。通过以这种方式构建提示，我们使视频语言模型能够有效地利用多视频信息，从而增强模型推理和回答与视频内容相关的查询的能力。

## 研究背景与问题

当前视频语言模型在全面视频推理方面面临重大挑战，主要是由于单个视频固有的时空信息不完整性，导致模型产生幻觉和不准确的理解。[1][2] 这些挑战源于以下因素：

1. **视觉感知限制**：稀疏采样、空间遮挡和视角切换等问题影响视频内容的完整捕捉 [1][4]
2. **理解能力限制**：视频内容复杂性和模型有效上下文长度的限制 [1][2]
3. **依赖语言先验**：当面对知识不完整时，模型往往依赖语言先验而非视觉事实 [1][2]

## 提出的解决方案

论文提出了一个结构化多视频协作推理框架，通过整合多个相关视频的信息来增强视频理解。[2][3] 该框架包含三个核心组件：

### 1. 视频结构化模块 (VSM)

该模块将视频表示为高效的时空图结构：[5][6]

- 使用场景检测减少视频的时间冗余
- 通过密集视频描述提取详细的文本概念
- 利用场景图解析器从描述中提取文本场景图（三元组形式）
- 基于图引导的目标检测与跟踪
- 建立包含节点（对象）、帧内边（空间关系）和帧间边（时间关系）的视频图表示

### 2. 图融合模块 (GFM)

该模块负责融合结构信息和多视频知识：[7][8]

- 利用层次化帧图注意力网络(HF-GAT)传递单视频内的结构信息
- 通过跨图注意力机制(CGA)从相关视频中选择和融合有用信息
- 将融合后的信息整合到增强的图节点令牌中

### 3. 结构化多视频提示设计

精心设计的提示模板整合图形令牌、视觉令牌和文本令牌，引导大语言模型进行推理。[8][9]

## 实验评估

### 数据集

- 在 VideoInstruct-100K 数据集的 10K 视频-文本指令子集上进行微调 [6]
- 在三个视频问答数据集上进行评估：ActivityNet-QA、MSRVTT-QA 和新建的 InternVid-QA [10][11]

### 主要结果

- 在 MSRVTT-QA 上提高 2.6%的准确率 [11]
- 在 ActivityNet-QA 上提高 1.6%的准确率 [11]
- 在 InternVid-QA 上提高 3.9%的准确率 [11]

### 消融研究

1. **组件有效性**：[12]

   - 视频结构化模块提高性能 0.5%
   - 多视频图融合进一步提高 3.4%
   - 结构化表示比直接拼接多视频令牌更有效

2. **图融合模块设计**：[12]

   - HF-GAT 提高性能 1.6%
   - CGA 进一步提高 1.7%

3. **相关视频影响**：[13][14]
   - 检索视频数量从 0 增加到 5 时性能提升，然后下降
   - 视频相关性越高，推理性能越好

## 主要贡献

1. 探索了一种可行的结构化推理框架，用于视频语言模型的多视频协作 [3]
2. 开发了视频结构化模块，获取数据高效的视频时空图表示 [3][5]
3. 设计了图融合模块，有效融合时空关系和跨视频知识 [3][7]
4. 通过大量实验证明了该框架能显著提升视频理解的可靠性和准确性 [3][14]

这一研究为解决视频理解中的信息不完整性问题提供了新的思路，通过结构化表示和多视频协作显著提升了视频语言模型的推理能力。[14]
